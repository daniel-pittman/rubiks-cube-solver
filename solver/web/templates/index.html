🎮 How to Use

  • Click Faces: Click on any cube face to rotate it
  • Right-Click: Use right-click for counter-clockwise rotations
  • Ctrl+Click: Double rotation (180°)
  • Rotate View: Drag background to rotate camera, scroll to zoom
  • Quick Actions: Use Scramble, Solve, and Reset buttons
  • Manual Moves: Enter moves like "R U R' U'" or use move buttons
  • Auto-Rotate: Click 🔄 button for automatic rotation
  • Reset View: Click 🏠 button to center the cube
